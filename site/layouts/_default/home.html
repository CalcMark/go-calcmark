Why CalcMark?

Variables flow downward

Define once, reference anywhere below. Change one number and watch everything update.

income = $5000           $5,000.00
rent = income * 0.30     $1,500.00
remaining = income - rent $3,500.00

Units are first-class

Write 5 miles in km or 20 celsius in fahrenheit. CalcMark understands physical units.

5 miles in km              8.05 km
20 celsius in fahrenheit   68 fahrenheit
3 gallons in liters        11.4 liters

Currencies built in

$100, 50 EUR, exchange rates via YAML frontmatter. Automatic formatting.

price = $1200      $1,200.00
price in EUR       €1,104.00
15% of price       $180.00

Markdown meets math

Write prose and calculations together. Your thinking stays in the document, right next to the numbers.

# Planning a trip
We need flights and a hotel.

budget = $2000       $2,000.00
spent = $1350        $1,350.00
remaining = budget - spent $650.00

Export anywhere

Convert to HTML, Markdown, JSON, or plain text. Pipe expressions from the command line.

$ cm convert budget.cm --to=html
$ cm convert budget.cm --to=json
$ cm convert budget.cm --to=markdown
$ cm convert budget.cm --to=text

Natural language

Read from storage, compress data, transfer across networks — in plain English.

read 100 MB from ssd       0.18 s
compress 1 GB using gzip   341 MB
1500000 as napkin          1.5M
