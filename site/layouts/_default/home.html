Why CalcMark?

Variables flow downward

Define once, reference anywhere below. Change one number and watch everything update.

Units are first-class

Write 5 miles in km or 20 celsius in fahrenheit. CalcMark understands physical units.

Currencies built in

$100, 50 EUR, exchange rates via YAML frontmatter. Automatic formatting.

Markdown is ignored

Write prose freely. Only lines that parse as calculations are evaluated. Your thinking stays in the document.

Export anywhere

Convert to HTML, Markdown, JSON, or plain text. Pipe expressions from the command line.

Terminal-native

A TUI editor that feels right at home in your terminal. Human-readable, diffable files.
